Meet Matthew

The short version.

Matthew Campoli studied history at York University — a BA in History — then spent seven years cutting hair, building a book of 200+ clients one chair at a time.

Real estate came next, about nine years ago. His first $1.2M deal came from a hair client — the same lesson that runs through everything since: people do business with people they trust.

Today he sells homes at THE AGENCY Toronto, hosts the Priced to Sell podcast, and co-founded The Coffee Party with Zohaib Aziz. He also sits on the advisory board at Hyyve. Top 35 Under 35 in Canadian real estate (2021), ranked in the top 2% of agents nationwide (2021).
